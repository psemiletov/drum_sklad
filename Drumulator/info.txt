The E-mu Drumulator is a sample-based drum machine introduced in 1983 by E-mu Systems. It features 12 8-bit samples. In 1984, Digidrums produced additional special EPROMs with more samples.

Notable users of the Drumulator include:
Cocteau Twins - on the albums Head over Heels, Sunburst and Snowblind, among others.
Depeche Mode - on the songs "Everything Counts", "Love, in Itself", and "Never Let Me Down Again".
Ultravox.
Front 242 = on the album No Comment (1984).
Jean-Michel Jarre = on Rendez-Vous (1986), specifically the track "Second Rendez-Vous".
