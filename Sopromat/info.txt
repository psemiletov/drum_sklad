# SOPROMAT (СОПРОМАТ)

## Credits
The original, raw sounds for this drumkit were generated using the algorithmic beat maker at https://dopeloop.ai/beat-maker/ (developed by Chris McCormick) based on ClojureScript. Subsequently, all samples were processed to create the final kit.

## Processing Details
- Converted from stereo to mono.
- Trimmed trailing silence with a micro fade-out.
