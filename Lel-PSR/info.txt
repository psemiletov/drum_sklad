LEL PSR (ЛЕЛЬ ПСР)

The legendary Soviet drum machine was developed in 1989 at the SAM factory (Счётно-аналитических машин) by Sergey Shershnev (Сергей Шершнев). At the end of the USSR, producers Andrei Tropillo and Andrei Vishnya recorded early albums by famous rock bands such as Kino, Strannye Igry, and Akvarium with the LEL drum machine (later Kino used a Yamaha RX-11).
